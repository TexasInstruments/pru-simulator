## uart-decode | UART decoder
uart-load: The first example is uart_print.asm. It sends the text Hello 2026 on output pin 0 at 115200 baud. Open it, click Load & Assemble, then Reset. || The first example is you art print. It sends the text, hello twenty twenty-six, on output pin zero, at one hundred fifteen thousand two hundred baud. Open it, click load and assemble, then reset.
uart-window: The UART decoder reads the recording of the Signal Graph. This text needs about 1740 samples, so set the graph window to 2048. || The you art decoder reads the recording of the signal graph. This text needs about seventeen hundred forty samples, so set the graph window to two thousand forty-eight.
uart-record: The UART card is at the bottom of Devices. Record starts the capture. It is the same recording as REC in the Signal Graph. || The you art card is at the bottom of devices. Record starts the capture. It is the same recording as rec in the signal graph.
uart-run: Click Run. The program sends its ten characters and halts on its own.
uart-stop: The button now reads Stop recording. Click it.
uart-decode: Decode detects the bit width in the GPO0 trace and shows the bytes as text: Hello 2026. || Click decode. It detects the bit width in the G P O zero trace, and shows the bytes as text: hello twenty twenty-six.
uart-clear: Clear empties the decoder output.

## uart-inject | UART RX Inject
inject-load: The second example is uart_rx_11frame.asm. It waits for frames of eleven bytes on input pin 0, at 4 megabaud. Load it and click Reset. || The second example is you art R X eleven frame. It waits for frames of eleven bytes on input pin zero, at four megabaud. Load it and click reset.
inject-mode: UART RX Inject drives such frames onto a pin. Hex and ASCII choose how you write the payload. Switching converts it. || You art R X inject drives such frames onto a pin. Hex and askey choose how you write the payload. Switching converts it.
inject-payload: Type the payload: HelloWorld!. The counter shows 11 bytes. || Type the payload: hello world, with an exclamation mark. The counter shows eleven bytes.
inject-settings: Pin offers GPI0 to GPI3. Baud is in megabaud. Frames is how many times the payload is sent. Leave them at GPI0, 4.00 and 1. || Pin offers G P I zero to G P I three. Baud is in megabaud. Frames is how many times the payload is sent. Leave them at G P I zero, four, and one.
inject-arm: Inject arms the generator. The status line reads: Armed, 11 bytes, 1 frame, run to receive. || Inject arms the generator. The status line reads: armed, eleven bytes, one frame, run to receive.
inject-run: Click Run. The frame arrives and the firmware stores it. Click Stop.
inject-memory: In the Simulator view, click Refresh in Memory 1 and choose Byte. Address 0 holds 48 65 6C 6C 6F, the letters of Hello. || In the simulator view, click refresh in memory one, and choose byte. Address zero holds forty-eight, sixty-five, six C, six C, six F, the letters of hello.
inject-reset: Reset inputs restores the form defaults and clears the status line. It does not cancel an armed injection.

## i2c-expander | I2C expander
i2c-load: The third example is i2c_tca9538_running_led.asm, an I2C master with SCL on pin 0 and SDA on pin 1. Load it and click Reset. || The third example is I two C, T C A ninety-five thirty-eight, running L E D: an I two C master with S C L on pin zero, and S D A on pin one. Load it and click reset.
i2c-name: The I2C expander card attaches a simulated TCA9538. Device name is its name in lists and events. I2C address takes the 7-bit address, here 0x23. || The I two C expander card attaches a simulated T C A ninety-five thirty-eight. Device name is its name in lists and events. I two C address takes the seven bit address, here hex twenty-three.
i2c-pins: SCL pin and SDA pin select the two bus pins, here 0 and 1. || S C L pin and S D A pin select the two bus pins, here zero and one.
i2c-attach: Click Attach expander. The card shows the eight pins, P0 on the left. They are all inputs until the firmware configures them. || Click attach expander. The card shows the eight pins, P zero on the left. They are all inputs until the firmware configures them.
i2c-run: Click Run. CONFIG becomes 0x00, so all pins are outputs, and the lit box walks from pin to pin. || Click run. The config register becomes hex zero zero, so all pins are outputs, and the lit box walks from pin to pin.
i2c-status: Below the boxes you see the OUTPUT, POLARITY and CONFIG registers, the counts of bus events and faults, and the last transaction. || Below the boxes you see the output, polarity and config registers, the counts of bus events and faults, and the last transaction.
i2c-list: Attached devices, below the cards, lists every device on this core with its wiring and a Detach button.
i2c-detach: Click Stop. Detach on the card removes the expander, from the card and from the list.
